FURLOUGH PUCK
=============
Round case for an NFC sticker. Two parts, no screws, no glue.

The last version fell off because the lid was only held by friction. This one
has a ring on the lid that clicks into a groove in the body.


PRINT THESE TWO
---------------
  1 - body.stl     open side UP
  2 - lid.stl      flat face DOWN  (don't flip it - the logo side goes on the bed)

  0.2mm layers, 4 walls, 20% infill
  No supports. No brim.
  PLA or PETG is fine.

  Don't use metal-filled or carbon-fibre filament. It stops the NFC working.


ASSEMBLE
--------
  1. Stick the NFC sticker inside the lid, centred on the flat part.
  2. Push the lid onto the body until it clicks.
  3. To get it back off: fingernail in the notch on the side.

Then tap a phone on the side with the logo. It should read from about half an
inch away. If that works, you're done.


IF THE LID ISN'T RIGHT
----------------------
Open  source/anchor-puck.scad  in OpenSCAD (free). Near the top you'll find:

      bead = 0.30;

  That number is how hard the lid grips. Stay between 0.10 and 0.40.

  Still pulls off          ->  go up by 0.05 or 0.10
  Won't push on, or the
  body cracks              ->  go down by 0.05 or 0.10

Then File > Export > STL. Do it twice - once with part set to "body", once
with part set to "lid". Both parts have to match, so always redo both.


OR SKIP THE HASSLE
------------------
The "twist version (backup)" folder has a different design - the lid drops on
and locks with a quarter turn, like a pill bottle. Nothing to adjust, it just
works. Print those two files instead if this one gives you trouble.

Thanks for testing it.
